📝 Answer

Q: Why should a sidebar be full-width on mobile but narrow on desktop?

On mobile, screens are small (<768px). If a sidebar takes only 25% of a tiny screen, it becomes too narrow to read or tap. Making it col-12 (full-width) lets users use the sidebar comfortably.

On desktop, there is plenty of space. Placing the sidebar as col-md-3 (25%) leaves 75% for main content (col-md-9). Both sit side-by-side without feeling cramped.

This is the core idea of responsive design — adapting layout based on available screen space.
